**Source:** the original Thinkific course "Introduction to Golden Lift Chairs and Advanced Power Recliners Sales Training:
Elevating Your Expertise" (course 2636309, 31 lessons). **Target:** HCPS Academy course "HCPS Lift Chair Sales & Product
Knowledge Certification", v1 (published, 17 lessons). Inventory read 22 Oct 2026 through the owner's signed-in Thinkific admin
session. It was read-only: slide panels were expanded, but nothing was saved, reordered, published or downloaded.
Lesson text and slide wording were already archived on 10 Oct 2026 (`thinkific-source-archive-2026-10-10.md`, kept outside
the public repo).

**Status keys:**
- **present**: the same media is in HCPS.
- **transformed**: the teaching is in HCPS in another form, such as text, an explorer or a comparison.
- **recoverable**: the media exists and can be added now.
- **owner file**: the original exists only in Thinkific or with the owner.
- **obsolete**: archived, draft or outdated.

## What can and cannot be recovered now

1. **Done in the draft (v2):** the 4 recoverable Golden videos, embedded in the matching lessons, each with a fallback link.
2. **21 HCPS-authored slides (lessons 1, 20, 21, 23, 24, 26, 27, 30):** these are images of the owner's own slides. They can
   be brought in as lesson images. The builder only imports from Golden's hosts, so this needs either:
   - the owner uploading his original slide files through Media → Upload; or
   - the owner's go-ahead to copy them from Thinkific's CDN (a one-time allow-list change).
3. **40 narration clips:** the Academy has no audio media kind. M3's block checker allows image / video / pdf / link only.
   Options, owner's choice:
   - (a) a small migration adding an `audio` kind, plus a narrated-slide block (slide image + audio player + transcript);
   - (b) combine each slide with its narration into a short video on HCPS's YouTube or Vimeo, embedded with the existing
     video block (no migration);
   - (c) transcribe the clips into text (loses the voice).

   I recommend (b) for speed or (a) for editability.
4. **31 Golden catalog slides (2024):** not re-imported. Several show discontinued models (CLAUDE.md §20). They are replaced
   by the current approved Golden photos already in the media library.
